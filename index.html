My design process is naturally a little chaotic, I collect references, experiment with different mediums, jump between styles and let unexpected ideas collide. What might look scattered at first often becomes the starting point for something new.

I’m drawn to storytelling, visual experimentation and the little details that make something feel uniquely its own. I enjoy exploring different areas of design rather than staying within one visual language, and I see that constant evolution as an important part of becoming a designer.

For me, chaos isn’t the absence of direction. It’s where curiosity begins.

Chalo Dilli! Photobook

A photobook about Delhi: its forts, markets, streets and people. I photographed the city, wrote the text and designed the book, with illustrations and cover lettering drawn in Illustrator.

Format
Photobook, A4 landscape spreads.
My role
Photography, writing, layout, illustration and cover lettering.
Tools
Adobe InDesign, Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop

Introduction

Delhi has always fascinated me — not just for its grand monuments or rich history, but for the way its culture lives and breathes in every street, every market, and every corner of the city.

This photobook is my attempt to capture that very spirit of Delhi — a city where the past and present beautifully co-exist. From centuries-old forts and tombs to vibrant street life and local traditions, Delhi’s heritage is everywhere — not just in its architecture, but in its people, its food, its colours, and its stories.

While exploring the city through my lens, I realised that Delhi isn’t just about visiting historical sites — it’s about experiencing its soul. It’s in the quiet moments at ancient shrines, the chaos of Sarojini, the art on forgotten walls, and the warmth of its people.

This book is a visual journey through the Delhi I experienced — raw, real, and timeless. A small tribute to a city that continues to inspire me every day.

The introduction page. I wrote the text; the elephant and the faint mandala behind it are my own drawings.

Illustrations

Before laying out the book I drew a set of motifs in Illustrator to tie the photographs together: a mandala printed faintly behind the introduction, an elephant heading the introduction page, and an auto-rickshaw.

Cover lettering

My first draft of the DELHI lettering didn’t work, so I started again. The final cover pairs a lowercase ‘chalo’ with a Devanagari-inspired DELHI.

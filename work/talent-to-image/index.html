Talent to Image: Rebranding Chef Erika Banduni

A rebranding shoot for chef and baker Erika Banduni, founder of CHOMP! microbakery. We built a social persona that puts her, not just her bread, at the centre of her feed, styled across three looks.

Client
Erika Banduni: chef, baker and founder of CHOMP! microbakery.
Team
Aadya, Aashi and Mohini.
Course
FD IDM: Fashion Visage. Social persona creation.

The brief

Who she is. A chef and baker whose speciality is sourdough made with Indian grains, alongside plant-based desserts. She teaches bread classes, runs pop-ups and builds community around plant-based food, local grains, slow food and craft.

The gap. Her Instagram showed her work more strongly than it showed her. Food filled the feed, with only the occasional portrait. We set out to make her the face of her brand: polished yet approachable, whimsical and craft-led.

The new image

Whimsical: unexpected colour, quirky accessories, playful details. Polished: clean silhouettes and considered layering, never stiff. Artisanal: natural fabrics, earthy colour, textures drawn from baking.

Colour palette

  • Oat#E8D9C5
  • Terracotta#A44F35
  • Butter#D9B94C
  • Sage#71815C
  • Cherry#D96C75

Look 01 · In the kitchen

A red tank, a printed neckerchief and an apron over denim, with graphic eyeliner and stacked rings and bangles.

Look 02 · Garden picnic

A floral top with puff sleeves, a white skirt and red roses in her hair, set against a picnic of her own bakes.

Look 03 · After hours

A black pinstripe blazer cinched with a pink belt, layered chains, a smoky eye and a red lip.

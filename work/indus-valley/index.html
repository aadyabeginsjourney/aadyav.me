Excavating the Garments of the Indus Valley Civilisation

Garments imagined from the figures of the Indus Valley Civilisation: the Priest-King of Mohenjo-daro and the terracotta mother goddess.

The looks
Priest-King: trefoil robe, headband, armband. Mother goddess: fan headdress, layered necklaces, girdle.
References
Sculpture and terracotta figurines from Indus Valley sites.
Shoot
Indoor, on a plain draped backdrop under warm light.

Priest-King

His look draws on the Priest-King, the stone figure found at Mohenjo-daro: a robe patterned with trefoils worn over one shoulder, a headband with a round ornament at the brow, and a matching armband.

Mother goddess

Her look comes from the terracotta mother goddess figurines: a fan-shaped headdress with cups at the sides, heavy layered necklaces and a girdle at the hips.

Behind the scenes

Fitting the headdress on set, and the team with both looks.

Graphene-Infused Innovative Textile Gym Bag

A research project in innovative textiles. I studied graphene as a material, analysed an existing graphene product, then applied what I learned to design a smart gym duffel bag.

Course
DS: Innovative Textiles
Process
Material research, case study, product design.
Material
Graphene-infused nylon
Material

What is graphene?

A single layer of carbon atoms arranged in a hexagonal structure: extremely thin, incredibly strong, and an excellent conductor of heat and electricity.

From material to wearable

Case study

Case study: Vollebak Graphene Jacket

Insulated jackets trap heat unevenly and hold odour. Graphene spreads body heat across the fabric for passive thermal equalisation, and adds anti-odour, lightweight strength.

Design

Smart gym duffel

Gym bags trap sweat, odour, moisture and bacteria. Graphene’s anti-bacterial, moisture-managing and thermal properties transfer straight into the bag.

1 Main compartment spacious, durable, thermally balanced.
2 Antimicrobial lining graphene lining stops odour and bacteria.
3 Temperature-sensitive pocket protects supplements and sensitive items.
4 Ventilated shoe compartment mesh plus antimicrobial lining.
5 USB / power strip conductive strip to charge from a power bank.
6 Exterior pockets quick access, water-repellent zips.

How it’s made

Production cost per bag INR 2,100–2,600. Estimated retail price INR 4,500–7,000: premium positioning, because of the graphene technology.
